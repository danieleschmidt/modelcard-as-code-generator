# bert-sentiment-amazon

BERT-base-uncased fine-tuned on Amazon product reviews for binary sentiment classification (positive / negative). Trained by the OpenClaw ML team as a reference example.

Model Details
  • **Version**: 1.2.0
  • **Authors**: Daniel Schmidt
  • **License**: Apache-2.0
  • **Base Model**: bert-base-uncased

    Intended Use Classify product reviews as positive or negative sentiment. Designed for use in e-commerce analytics pipelines. Target users: data science teams at retail companies. NOT intended for mental health, medical, or legal applications.

    Training Details
  • **Framework**: PyTorch
  • **Training Data**: amazon-reviews-v2 (2.4M reviews, balanced 50/50 split)
  • **Hyperparameters**: - learning_rate: 2e-05 - batch_size: 32 - epochs: 4 - warmup_ratio: 0.1 - weight_decay: 0.01 - max_length: 256

    Evaluation Results
  • **accuracy**: 0.942 (CI: [0.939, 0.945])
  • **f1**: 0.941
  • **precision**: 0.938
  • **recall**: 0.944
  • **roc_auc**: 0.987
  • **accuracy**: 0.891

    Ethical Considerations ### Bias Risks
  • Model accuracy is 3–5 pp lower on reviews written in non-standard English.
  • Negative-sentiment reviews by minority demographic groups may be under-detected. ### Fairness Metrics
  • **demographic_parity_ratio**: 0.96
  • **equalized_odds_difference**: 0.04

    Limitations
  • Trained on Amazon reviews; may not generalise to other review domains.
  • Accuracy drops ~5 pp on very short reviews (< 20 words).
  • Does not handle mixed-sentiment or neutral reviews well.
  • English-only; multilingual reviews will produce unreliable results.

    Carbon Footprint Training was tracked with CodeCarbon. Estimated emissions: **1.2 kg CO₂e** (equivalent to ~5 km driven). Hardware: 2x A100, ~6 hours @ ~250W each.
